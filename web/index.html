Mount Kenya Wildfire Risk
How to read this map The colours rank squares from lowest to highest relative risk. They do not say a fire will start: most Very High squares have no fire in the next 8 days. The model sees dryness, heat and terrain, but not ignition sources such as people. Use it to decide where to look first. The top-20 list ranks squares within the chosen period, and the table shows how often a new fire followed each colour in back-testing on years the model never saw.
